# Evidence-driven development checkpoint

CDD fixed the multi-VM fleet, envelope, trial, evidence, policy, authority, and response contracts before implementation. TDD captured RED gates for three distinct VM identities, baseline/migration/disruption trial completeness, envelope breaches, correlation gaps, direct live-evidence requirements, restartable evidence, packaging, and a zero-authority handoff. EDD connects each claim to produced evidence.

- JSON Schema examples cover `ALLOW_REVIEW`, `REFUSE`, and `ABSTAIN`.
- The adapter evaluates supplied current-session measurements and never authors performance values.
- `LIVE` requires fresh OpenShift, KubeVirt, every VM, migration, disruption, CPU placement, inference, telemetry, and continuity evidence.
- The durable JSONL ledger is reloaded after restart.
- Helm renders three VM members, a PVC-backed non-root/read-only adapter, a read-only presentation image, tokenless service accounts, default-deny networking, and Secret references.
- Browser checks exercise all top-level scenes, every architecture reveal, all live conditions, keyboard controls, explicit close, and no-scroll fit at 1920×1080 and 1440×900.
- The separate Showroom lab requires qualification, restart, and zero residue in 75–90 minutes.
- The release workflow builds Linux/AMD64 images, requires zero High/Critical findings, generates SPDX SBOMs, OIDC-signs, attaches SLSA provenance, and proves exact-digest pull.

Factory evidence is `REHEARSAL`. Launchpad owns target `LIVE`, seat/capacity, cleanup, certification, and promotion evidence.
