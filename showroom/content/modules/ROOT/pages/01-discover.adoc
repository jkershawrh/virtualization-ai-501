= 1. DISCOVER — 10 minutes

This is the opening step of the 75–90 minutes fleet-qualification journey.

== Show

Open the OpenShift Console *VirtualMachines* view. The lab declares three
clients—`inference-client-a`, `-b`, and `-c`—plus a qualification adapter
and durable evidence PVC. Inspect both *VirtualMachines* and
*Storage → PersistentVolumeClaims* in the Console.

== Learn

Three Running VMs are topology, not proof of inference, Intel CPU placement, or
qualification. Each of those claims needs current, correlated evidence.

== Do

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
oc get virtualmachines,virtualmachineinstances -n "$LAB_NAMESPACE" -o wide
----

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
oc get deployment,service,persistentvolumeclaims -n "$LAB_NAMESPACE" \
  -l app.kubernetes.io/instance=virtualization-ai-501
----

== Prove

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
test "$(oc get virtualmachines -n "$LAB_NAMESPACE" -o name | wc -l | tr -d ' ')" -eq 3
----

Missing OpenShift or OpenShift Virtualization versions remain blockers; do not
fill them with assumptions.
