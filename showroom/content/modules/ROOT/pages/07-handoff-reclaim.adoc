= 7. HANDOFF and participant cleanup — 9 minutes

== Show

The durable ledger lets a reviewer retrieve the complete qualification evidence
by request identifier.

== Learn

Participants remove only local fixture/result files. Launchpad owns namespace reclamation
and zero residue verification. Adapter restart and PVC continuity
are platform certification checks, not participant mutations. Do not uninstall the lab, delete its
namespace, shared operators, CRDs, nodes, storage classes, or Secrets.

== Do

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
REQUEST_ID="$(jq -r .request_id /tmp/fleet-qualified-result.json)"
curl -fsS "$QUAL_URL/api/v1/qualifications/$REQUEST_ID" | tee /tmp/fleet-ledger.json | jq '{request_id,states:[.evidence[].state],records:(.evidence|length)}'
----

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
curl -fsS "$QUAL_URL/metrics" | grep '^virtualization_ai_501_'
----

== Prove

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
jq -e '.evidence | length > 0' /tmp/fleet-ledger.json
rm -f /tmp/fleet-{qualified,capacity,gap}{,-result}.json /tmp/fleet-ledger.json
----

The handoff is evidence for independent human review, not Launchpad
certification or promotion. It proves the 401 single-operation boundary can be
evaluated at fleet scale without granting the adapter authority.
